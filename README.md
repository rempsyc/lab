## About the lab

The SAGE Lab studies how people understand themselves, care for others, and navigate collective life. Directed by [Rémi Thériault](https://remi-theriault.com/), the lab brings together social psychology, contemplative traditions, and open science to investigate **robust prosociality**: positive change that is meaningful, durable, and supported by reliable evidence.

Our work is organized around three complementary research areas:

- **Robust prosociality:** identity, empathy, polarization, cooperation, and interventions designed to foster lasting social change.
- **Well-being:** mindfulness, self-understanding, resilience, and the conditions that support durable personal change.
- **Robust science:** open, reproducible, inclusive, and collaborative approaches to behavioural research.

We use methods including experiments, longitudinal interventions, artificial intelligence, virtual reality, big data, contemplative practices, and open-source software.
