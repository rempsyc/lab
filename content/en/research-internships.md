The SAGE Lab considers student internship inquiries in nonclinical psychology research. Possible projects depend on the fit with our current research and the supervision available during your proposed dates. If your program requires a clinical placement, start with your program’s placement coordinator.

## Before contacting us

1. Review the SAGE Lab’s research themes and projects. Identify a question or project you’d like to contribute to and what you hope to learn.
2. Check what your home program requires. Include any fixed dates, required hours, an internship agreement, and forms your university expects the host to sign.
3. Use the contact page to send a short inquiry with your program and institution, proposed start and end dates, project interests, and any funding you have or plan to apply for. Attach your CV. If available, include a link to your program’s internship requirements.

[Research themes]({{< relref "research.md" >}}) · [Project portfolio](../projects/) · [Contact the SAGE Lab]({{< relref "contact.md" >}})

## Follow UQAR’s internship process

UQAR’s first step is to identify a professor who can supervise the internship. If a professor agrees to host you, follow UQAR’s instructions for the internship agreement and research-intern admission. For applicants coming from abroad, UQAR’s page explains how to work with the International Relations Office and recommends starting the process about six months before the proposed start date.

[UQAR’s practical and research internship procedures (French)](https://www.uqar.ca/recherche/releve-scientifique-recherchee/stages-pratiques-ou-de-recherche/) · [Internship and graduate research opportunities at UQAR (French)](https://www.uqar.ca/recherche/releve-scientifique-recherchee/)

If you’re already studying at UQAR and want to get involved in research, start with our guide to research opportunities. For a postdoctoral appointment, use the postdoc guide.
