SAGE welcomes inquiries from students, researchers, and community partners whose interests connect with self, altruism, groups, or empathy.

## Students

If you are considering research training at UQAR, tell us about your background, the questions that interest you, and how they connect with the lab’s themes.

## Researchers and partners

We are open to collaborations that bring complementary perspectives, methods, or community knowledge to our work.

Current opportunities will be posted on this page. You can also [contact the lab](../contact/) to introduce yourself.
