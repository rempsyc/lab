SAGE studies how people understand themselves, care for others, and behave within groups. Our work is organized around four connected themes.

## Self

How do identity, self-concept, and self-regulation shape the way people think and act?

## Altruism

What motivates helping, cooperation, compassion, and other forms of prosocial behaviour?

## Groups

How do belonging, shared identities, social norms, and intergroup relations influence people and communities?

## Empathy

How do people understand and respond to others’ experiences, and how can empathy help bridge social divides?

Across these themes, the lab values transparent, reproducible, and open research practices.
