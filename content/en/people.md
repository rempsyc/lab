## Rémi Thériault, PhD

**Lab Director · Assistant Professor of Psychology**  
Université du Québec à Rimouski (UQAR)

Rémi Thériault is an Assistant Professor of Psychology at UQAR and director of the SAGE Lab. His research uses social and personality psychology to understand how people relate to themselves, to one another, and to the groups around them.

His work has examined social identities, implicit cognition, prosociality, empathy, self-regulation, embodiment, and political polarization. He is also committed to open science, reproducible research, metascience, and data science using R.

Before joining UQAR, Rémi completed a postdoctoral fellowship in social psychology at New York University. He earned a PhD in Psychology from the Université du Québec à Montréal, as well as an MSc in Psychiatry and a BA in Psychology from McGill University.

Four themes from the Vipassanā contemplative tradition have influenced his approach and research interests: morality (*sīla*), mindfulness (*samādhi*), wisdom (*paññā*), and loving-kindness (*mettā*). He also maintains a public [Research Garden](https://rempsyc.github.io/research-ideas/) of research ideas, ongoing projects, essays, and conceptual maps.

### Research interests

- Social identities and group processes
- Prosociality, altruism, and empathy
- Implicit cognition and self-regulation
- Political polarization and shared identities
- Morality, mindfulness, and loving-kindness
- Open and reproducible science
